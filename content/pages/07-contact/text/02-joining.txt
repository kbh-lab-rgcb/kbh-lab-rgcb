title: Joining the lab

We are glad to hear from prospective PhD students, project staff and
post-doctoral researchers. Write to Dr. Harikumar with a CV, a short statement
of what you want to work on, and the names of two referees.

PhD admissions run through the BRIC-RGCB and national fellowship routes
(CSIR-UGC NET, DBT-JRF, ICMR-JRF). Mention any fellowship you already hold.
