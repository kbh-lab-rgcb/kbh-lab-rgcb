# Banner images

**Drop an image in this folder and it becomes the banner across the top of this
page.** Any name, any size. No other step.

- **One image** → a still banner.
- **Two or more** → they turn into a slideshow automatically, with arrows and
  dots. Visitors can also swipe on a phone.
- **None** → a plain coloured banner. The page still works.

Order is alphabetical, so name them `01-…`, `02-…` to control the sequence.

Accepted: `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.tif` `.tiff` `.svg` `.gif`

Big photos straight from a phone, a camera or a microscope are fine — they are
resized automatically when the site is built. Landscape images work best;
something around 1600 × 900 or wider is ideal. Microscopy `.tiff` files can be
dropped in as they come off the instrument: they are converted to JPEG for the
web automatically, because no browser can display TIFF.

**Do not convert a photograph to `.svg`.** SVG is for drawings and diagrams. A
photo "converted" to SVG is just the picture buried inside a vector wrapper —
usually shrunk in the process — and the site has to publish that wrapper whole,
so it ends up both blurrier and many times heavier. Save photographs as `.jpg`,
or leave the `.tiff` as it is.

The `.svg` files here now are placeholders. Delete them once you have real
photographs.
