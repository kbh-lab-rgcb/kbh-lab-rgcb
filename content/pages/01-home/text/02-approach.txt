title: How we work

We pair genetic mouse models with primary immune cell biology, lipidomics, and
patient-derived material, and we follow the biology wherever it leads — from
autoimmunity to pancreatic, colorectal and liver cancer. The lab sits within the
Cancer Research Programme at BRIC-Rajiv Gandhi Centre for Biotechnology, and we
collaborate widely, in India and abroad.

We train students to ask a sharp question, design the experiment that could
falsify it, and write clearly about the result.
