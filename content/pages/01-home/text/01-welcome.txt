title: Inflammation, resolved

Inflammation is how the body answers injury and infection. Switching that answer
off again — *resolution* — is just as active a process, and just as tightly
controlled. When resolution fails, inflammation persists, and persistent
inflammation is one of the surest routes to cancer.

Our laboratory works on the lipid signals that govern this switch. We study
**sphingosine 1-phosphate (S1P)** and the enzymes that make and break it, asking
how they steer the innate immune response, how tumours co-opt them to build a
permissive microenvironment, and whether they can be drugged.
