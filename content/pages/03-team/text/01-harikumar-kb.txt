name: Dr. K.B. Harikumar
role: Scientist F · Principal Investigator
email: harikumar@rgcb.res.in
focus: Sphingolipid signalling in inflammation, immunity and cancer

Harikumar leads the laboratory's work on the role of inflammation in physiology
— the innate immune response — and in pathophysiology, principally cancer.

He took his PhD in Biochemistry at Amala Cancer Research Centre, Thrissur, in
2008, after an MSc from Nagpur University. He was a research intern at the
UT MD Anderson Cancer Center and a post-doctoral associate at Virginia
Commonwealth University before returning to India as a DBT Ramalingaswami
Fellow. He has been at BRIC-RGCB since, as Scientist C, Scientist E-I and now
Scientist F.

He is a member of the Indian Association for Cancer Research, the American
Association of Indian Scientists in Cancer Research, and the European Federation
of Biotechnology.
