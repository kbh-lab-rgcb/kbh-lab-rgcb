title: Joining the lab

We are glad to hear from prospective PhD students, project staff and
post-doctoral researchers. Write to Dr. Harikumar with a CV, a short statement of what you want to work on, and the names of two referees.

**PhD admissions** run through the BRIC-RGCB and national fellowship routes
(CSIR-UGC NET, DBT-JRF, ICMR-JRF). There are 2 admission cycles per year. Look for the notification on the BRIC-RGCB website. 

**For short-term training**, check the link section and apply through the BRIC-RGCB website. 

**For consultation**, look for the consultation Link and check the available consultation services and price. Any additional or custom consultation and services can be availed within the limitations of the institute and lab. For such queries, you can email Dr. K. B Harikumar. 

All face-to-face consultations or meetings require an appointment in advance.
