title: Sphingolipid signalling in innate immunity

Sphingosine kinases 1 and 2 (SphK1, SphK2) generate S1P, and S1P acting through
its receptors is a central regulator of immune cell trafficking and activation.

We showed that SphK1 regulates the TLR7/9-dependent type I interferon response,
placing the enzyme upstream of a pathway that drives autoimmunity, and examined
the distinct contribution of SphK2 in a murine model of systemic lupus
erythematosus. More recently we have reviewed how S1P signalling operates across
infection and immunity more broadly.
