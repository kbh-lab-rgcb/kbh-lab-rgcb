title: Overview
caption: The programme's research themes, and the agencies supporting them.

Our central theme is the **cellular dynamics of resolving inflammation, with
special reference to the role of sphingolipids**. Sphingolipids are not merely
structural membrane components: sphingosine 1-phosphate and ceramide act as
signalling molecules that set the tone of an immune response and, in tumours,
the character of the microenvironment.

Work in the lab moves between three questions — how these lipids shape innate
immunity, how tumours exploit them, and whether the enzymes that produce them
are tractable drug targets. Alongside the experimental work we have reviewed how
S1P signalling operates across infection and immunity, the place of S1PR1 in
cancer, and the role of phytochemicals in cancer immunotherapy.

The [research stories](../stories/) follow individual lines of that work, from
an initial observation to a sharper biological or translational conclusion.
