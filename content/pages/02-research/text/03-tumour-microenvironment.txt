title: The tumour microenvironment

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma is defended by a dense desmoplastic stroma that
keeps chemotherapy out. We found that disrupting the **S1PR1/STAT3 feed-forward
loop** breaks down that desmoplasia and re-sensitises pancreatic tumours to
gemcitabine, and we continue to work on tumour-associated macrophages as the
cells that hold this loop together.

In colorectal cancer we target the other side of the pathway: inhibiting **acid
ceramidase** shifts the balance of the microenvironment and enhances the
antitumour immune response.
