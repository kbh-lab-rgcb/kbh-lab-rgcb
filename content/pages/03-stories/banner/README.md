# Banner

**Put an image here and it becomes the strip across the top of the page.**

One image gives a still banner. **Two or more turn into a slideshow
automatically**, with arrows and dots.

Upload the full-size original — it is resized for you. Use a photo at least
1200 pixels wide, or it will look soft stretched across the page.

There is no banner on this page yet, so it shows a plain gradient. That works,
but a picture is better.

Accepted: `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.svg` `.gif`
