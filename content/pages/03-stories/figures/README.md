# Figures

**A figure here is paired with the story of the same name.**

```text
stories/03-pancreatic-stroma.txt
figures/03-pancreatic-stroma.jpg
```

The names must match apart from the file extension. The number prefix must match
too, so renumbering a story means renaming its picture as well.

Each picture is used twice: beside the opening of the story on this page, and as
the background of that story's card on the home page, where it sits under a dark
wash so the writing on top stays readable.

If no figure matches, the story is simply full width and its card on the home
page is plain. Nothing breaks.

Add a caption by putting a `caption:` line in the story file:

```text
title: Breaking the stromal barrier to pancreatic cancer treatment
caption: Collagen laid down between the acini, in blue.
```

Upload the full-size original — it is resized for you.

Accepted: `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.svg` `.gif`
